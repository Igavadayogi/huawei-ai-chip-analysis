## Post Copy

Huawei claims $12B in AI chip revenue for 2026.

The source? Anonymous. Unverifiable from their own filings.

So I ran five independent checks — competitor earnings, unit shipment data, named buyer commitments, manufacturing constraints, and demand driver analysis.

The number the data actually supports: **$8–10B.**

Still the biggest displacement of Western chip revenue in AI history. Still an extraordinary story. Just not quite the headline.

But the most useful thing I found wasn't the revenue range. It was this:

Nvidia's own SEC filings tell you more about Huawei's trajectory than Huawei does. When China drops from 25% of your data centre revenue to essentially zero — the demand doesn't disappear. It redirects.

The claim might be 20% optimistic. The direction is unambiguous.

That gap between what a headline says and what data shows — that's where analysis actually lives.
